What did the experiment establish?

Ten designed scenarios and four gate configurations produced 44 dispatch attempts. The validator always approves. Only an append to an in-memory ledger counts as execution.

The signed restricted fixture commits under approval-only and token-only, but policy-enabled modes deny it. In the forged-capability fixture, policy permits the scope; signature verification supplies the block. The authorized twin commits in every mode.

No live model was tested. This is a single-process software demonstration, not an isolated production executor.
