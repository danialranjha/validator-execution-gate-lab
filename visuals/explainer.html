An AI agent may write notes to inbox, but not to restricted. Assume an adversary tricks its verifier agent through prompt injection into granting signed approval to write to restricted. The signature passes; the independent inbox-only policy still blocks the write.

Without that policy, the restricted write executes. With it, the restricted write is blocked while a valid, approved inbox write still succeeds.

The simulation starts after the assumed compromise: forced approval and a synthetic signing key supply the valid signed request. No live verifier or prompt-injection attack was tested. The executor and policy remain trusted.
